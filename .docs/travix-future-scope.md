# Travix — Future Scope & Roadmap

_Where the application can grow_

## Why this document exists

Travix today covers the essentials: users sign up, describe a trip, get an AI-generated
itinerary with a budget estimate, edit it, and see hotel suggestions. This is a solid
foundation — but intentionally a focused one. The current business logic is deliberately
lean so it can be built well and shipped fast.

This document captures the many directions Travix can expand. None of these are required
for the application to work; they describe how a simple, single-purpose planner can grow
into a complete travel platform.

---

## 1. Smarter, deeper itineraries

- **Time-aware scheduling** — plan activities by hour (morning/afternoon/evening), not
  just by day, accounting for opening hours and travel time between stops.
- **Distance & routing optimization** — group nearby attractions together to minimize
  back-and-forth travel.
- **Weather-aware planning** — swap outdoor activities for indoor ones when rain is
  forecast.
- **Local events & seasonality** — surface festivals, concerts, and seasonal highlights
  happening during the travel dates.
- **Pace preferences** — "relaxed" vs "packed" trips that adjust how much is scheduled
  per day.

## 2. Booking & transactions

- **Flight search & booking** integration with airline / aggregator APIs.
- **Hotel booking** directly from the suggestions, not just recommendations.
- **Activity & ticket booking** for tours, museums, and experiences.
- **Restaurant reservations** tied to itinerary stops.
- **Payment processing** and order history within the app.

This turns Travix from a _planning_ tool into a _booking_ platform — the largest
business opportunity.

## 3. Collaboration & social features

- **Group trips** — multiple users planning the same trip together with shared edits.
- **Itinerary sharing** via public links or invitations.
- **Comments & voting** on activities within a group.
- **Community templates** — browse and clone itineraries other travelers have created.
- **Reviews & ratings** contributed by Travix users.

## 4. Personalization & intelligence

- **Travel profiles** that learn from past trips and saved preferences.
- **Personalized recommendations** based on previous destinations and ratings.
- **Dietary, accessibility, and mobility filters** baked into every suggestion.
- **Loyalty & rewards** awareness (airline miles, hotel points).
- **Budget tracking** during the trip — compare estimated vs actual spend.

## 5. Mobile & on-the-go experience

- **Native mobile apps** (iOS / Android) for travelers on the move.
- **Offline access** to itineraries without internet.
- **Real-time notifications** — flight delays, check-in reminders, "next stop" nudges.
- **Live map view** of the day's plan.
- **Currency & language helpers** for international trips.

## 6. Expanded AI capabilities

- **Conversational planning** — refine trips through natural chat instead of forms.
- **Voice input** for hands-free planning.
- **Image-based inspiration** — "plan a trip like this photo."
- **Multi-destination & multi-city** trip planning.
- **Smart re-planning** — automatically adjust the rest of the trip when one part
  changes (a delay, a cancellation, a new interest).

## 7. Business & monetization paths

- **Subscription tiers** (free vs premium planning features).
- **Affiliate revenue** from bookings and referrals.
- **B2B offering** for travel agencies and corporate travel.
- **Sponsored placements** for hotels, tours, and local businesses.
- **White-label** version of Travix for partners.

## 8. Platform & operational growth

- **Multi-currency and multi-language** support for a global audience.
- **Analytics dashboard** for users (trip history, spending trends).
- **Admin & content management** tools for the operating team.
- **Third-party API access** so other apps can build on Travix.
- **Scalable infrastructure** to support a growing user base reliably.

---

## How to think about the roadmap

These features fall into rough priority bands:

| Band          | Theme                     | Examples                                                          |
| ------------- | ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Near term** | Make the core better      | Time-aware itineraries, weather awareness, conversational editing |
| **Mid term**  | Turn planning into action | Hotel & flight booking, group collaboration, mobile app           |
| **Long term** | Become a platform         | Marketplace, B2B, white-label, API ecosystem                      |

The key point: **the current application is a strong, intentionally simple foundation.**
Each item above can be added independently and incrementally, allowing Travix to scale
from a personal trip planner into a full-featured travel platform without rebuilding what
already exists.
