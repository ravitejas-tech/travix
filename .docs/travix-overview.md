# Travix — Travel Intelligence Experience

*A non-technical overview*

## What is Travix?

Travix is a web application that plans your trips for you. Instead of spending hours
researching destinations, comparing prices, and piecing together a day-by-day schedule,
you simply tell Travix where you want to go and what you enjoy — and an AI travel
assistant builds a complete, personalized itinerary in seconds.

Think of it as having a knowledgeable travel agent available 24/7, one that listens to
your preferences and produces a ready-to-use plan you can adjust whenever you like.

## Who is it for?

Anyone planning a trip:

- **Busy travelers** who don't have time to research.
- **First-time visitors** to a city or country who don't know where to start.
- **People on a budget** who want a clear cost estimate before committing.
- **Spontaneous explorers** who want ideas tailored to their interests.

Every user has their own private account. Your trips, plans, and details are visible
only to you — no one else can see or change them.

## How it works (in plain steps)

### 1. Create your account
You sign up and log in securely. Your personal dashboard is your home base, showing
the trips you've planned.

### 2. Tell Travix about your trip
You fill in a short form with:

- **Destination** — e.g. Tokyo
- **Number of days** — e.g. 5 days
- **Budget level** — Low, Medium, or High
- **Interests** — Food, Culture, Adventure, Shopping, and more

### 3. Get an instant itinerary
The AI assistant creates a **day-by-day plan** built around what you care about.

> **Day 1**
> - Visit Senso-ji Temple
> - Explore Asakusa street food
>
> **Day 2**
> - Tokyo Skytree
> - Akihabara shopping

### 4. See an estimated budget
Travix also estimates what the trip will cost, broken down so there are no surprises.

> **Estimated Budget**
> - Flights: $400
> - Accommodation: $300
> - Food: $150
> - Activities: $100
> - **Total: $950**

### 5. Make it your own
The plan is a starting point, not a rulebook. You can:

- **Remove** an activity you're not interested in.
- **Add** something you'd rather do.
- **Regenerate a day** with new ideas — for example, *"Regenerate Day 3 with more
  outdoor activities."*

### 6. Find a place to stay (bonus)
Travix can also suggest hotels for your destination, matched to your budget and based
on popular traveler ratings.

> **Recommended Hotels**
> - Hotel Sakura Tokyo — Budget Friendly
> - Shinjuku Grand Hotel — Mid Range
> - Tokyo Imperial Palace Hotel — Luxury

## What makes Travix useful

- **Saves time** — a full plan in seconds instead of hours of research.
- **Personalized** — every itinerary reflects your interests and budget.
- **Flexible** — change anything, anytime.
- **Transparent costs** — know roughly what you'll spend before you go.
- **Private and secure** — your travel plans belong to you alone.

## The big picture

Travix turns a vague idea — *"I want to visit Tokyo for a few days"* — into a concrete,
adjustable plan with activities, costs, and places to stay. It removes the stress from
trip planning so you can focus on the part that matters: the journey itself.
